Overview

US Stocks is INDmoney's flagship product — the primary way millions of Indian investors access US equities. I own Trading Experience, Taxation & Compliance for the product: the end-to-end journey across three broker partners (DriveWealth, Alpaca, and NSEIX), for an account base of 3M+ users and $1.5B+ in AUM.

The challenge

Monthly trading volume had plateaued around $150M. Growth wasn't a marketing or acquisition problem — the accounts and AUM were already there. The gap was in the trading experience itself: users who wanted to trade more actively, at more sophisticated hours and with more control, didn't have the tools to do it inside the app.

My approach

  • Identified that a meaningful share of engaged users were price-sensitive to US market hours (a 9.5-hour timezone gap from India) and wanted to react to pre/post-market moves.
  • Prioritized and shipped Extended Hour Trading, giving users a trading window aligned to when US market-moving news actually breaks.
  • Shipped Technical Charts to give more active traders the charting tools they needed to make in-app decisions, rather than relying on external tools.
  • Shipped Advanced Order Types, closing a gap against dedicated trading apps for users who wanted more control over execution.
  • Coordinated across three broker integrations (DriveWealth, Alpaca, NSEIX) and Compliance to make sure every new capability was compliant across account types before rollout.
Also led parallel workstreams on US IPOs, Tax Loss Harvesting, and a Redemption Tax Calculator — extending the same trading experience into tax-efficient investing use cases.

Impact

Monthly trading volume scaled from $150M to $1B — a 6.7x increase — driven primarily by adoption of Extended Hour Trading and Advanced Order Types among the platform's most active traders.

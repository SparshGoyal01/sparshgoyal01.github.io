Overview

InCred's Personal Loans business relied on Lending Service Providers (LSPs) and Direct Selling Agents (DSAs) to originate loans, but the application journey behind that channel was largely manual and disconnected — slow for the customer and hard to scale for the business. I owned building Omnichannel PL: an end-to-end digital journey for these partner channels, from concept to launch.

The challenge

Median loan application turnaround time was 3 days. Every day of TAT was a day where a customer could drop off, go to a competitor, or simply lose interest — and it capped how fast the LSP/DSA channel could scale disbursals. The journey needed to move from a largely offline, partner-mediated process to a digital one, without breaking the partner relationships the channel depended on.

My approach

  • Mapped the existing offline/partner-assisted journey end-to-end to find where time was actually being lost — document collection, underwriting handoffs, and manual verification steps.
  • Designed and shipped a digital, omnichannel application flow that LSP and DSA partners could originate through, while keeping the customer-facing experience consistent across channels.
  • Worked with Engineering and Design to take the journey from concept through to launch, owning scope and sequencing across the build.
  • Partnered with Business, Marketing, Operations, Compliance, and Customer Support to define the launch plan, coordinate rollout, and monitor adoption once live.
This was one of two major initiatives in the same period — I also launched SENP, a GST/ITR-based underwriting product for SME owners, which reached ₹50 Cr+ in monthly disbursals within 6 months of launch.

Impact

Median application TAT dropped from 3 days to 1 day — a 65% reduction — directly contributing to the PL business scaling from ₹100 Cr to ₹450 Cr+ in monthly disbursals over the same period, profitably.
